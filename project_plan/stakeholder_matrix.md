# Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Information need | Engagement |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Studio owner | High | High | Revenue, profit, cash, priorities | Weekly decision review |
| Photographer | High | Medium | Demand, workload, package mix | Biweekly operations review |
| Administrative assistant | High | Medium | balances, response time, exceptions | Daily queue and weekly metrics |
| Marketing contractor | High | Medium | source conversion and revenue | Monthly channel review |
| Clients | Medium | Low | clear scheduling and payment steps | Automated timely updates |
