# Executive Summary

> Independent portfolio case study using synthetic data inspired by common photography-business workflows.

The synthetic dataset indicates $638,507 in cash revenue against $177,620 in operating expenses, producing $460,887 in gross profit and a 72.2% portfolio-level margin under the case-study cost assumptions. The average booking value is $857. Conversion from the 1,800 generated leads to 900 bookings is 50.0%; the repeat-client rate is 33.1%. The operational exceptions are material: $132,583 remains outstanding, response time averages 36.7 hours, cancellations are 7.7%, reschedules are 8.9%, and 78.0% of booking records have a completed session.

Wedding Collection leads package revenue at $305,886 and gross profit at $171,336. Instagram contributes the most bookings (219) and attributed revenue ($153,149), followed by Google Search and Referral. The analysis suggests prioritizing response-time discipline, payment reminders, and channel decisions based on conversion and value—not lead volume alone.
