# Recommendations

The analysis suggests a fictional studio could:

1. Set a 24-business-hour first-response target and route unanswered inquiries into a daily exception queue.
2. Require a signed agreement and deposit before confirmed status; automate reminders at defined intervals for remaining balances.
3. Protect high-demand October–November and April–May capacity with earlier campaign and staffing plans.
4. Preserve Wedding Collection as the premium revenue anchor while testing pricing and bundles for Essential Portrait and Brand Builder.
5. Increase or maintain investment only after comparing channel conversion, attributed revenue, and marketing cost; do not optimize solely for lead count.
6. Add cancellation/reschedule reason codes and review them monthly to distinguish preventable process failures from client circumstances.
7. Treat the forecast as a planning range and retrain after at least another year of synthetic or legitimately sourced operational history.

These are portfolio recommendations only; they were not implemented by a real company.
