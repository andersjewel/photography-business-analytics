# Findings

> Independent portfolio case study using synthetic data inspired by common photography-business workflows.

1. Wedding Collection is the revenue and gross-profit leader; Essential Portrait is the strongest non-wedding package by revenue.
2. Weddings produce the most service revenue and gross profit, while lower-overhead portrait categories retain attractive modeled contribution margins.
3. Instagram generates the most attributed bookings and revenue. Google Search and Referral form the next-highest-volume group.
4. Overall lead-to-booking conversion is 50.0%. Channel-specific decisions should compare all generated leads to bookings in the BI model, not booking share alone.
5. 33.1% of booked clients appear more than once in the booking file.
6. Average booking value is $857.
7. Cancellation and rescheduling rates are 7.7% and 8.9%.
8. October–November and graduation-season spikes are visible; January is the weakest recurring period. A small January 2026 amount comes from December inquiries booked after year-end.
9. Inquiry-to-booking time averages 12.3 days; initial response averages 36.7 hours.
10. Outstanding synthetic balances total $132,583 and are listed at booking level in CSV, SQL, and Excel.
11. Wedding Collection has the strongest combination of revenue and modeled profitability.
12. Slow first response, open balances, outcome changes, and session-record exceptions are the clearest workflow bottlenecks.
13. Instagram, Search, and Referral warrant disciplined tests; Paid Advertisement should be evaluated against spend before expansion.
14. The six-month forecast is a directional seasonal-trend baseline, not a production forecast.
15. Standardized intake, response SLAs, deposit rules, reminders, and exception queues may reduce cancellations and manual work.
